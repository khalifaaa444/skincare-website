The flower

Gathered at sunrise, petals still cool.

Our peonies are cut in the first hours of light, when every petal is full of water, and pressed before noon. The extract goes straight into the cream.

The cream

One cream, three promises.

  1. i

    Deep, lasting moisture

    Hyaluronic acid draws water in and holds it there all day.

  2. ii

    A smoother surface

    Fine lines look softer after a month of morning and night use.

  3. iii

    Calmer, even skin

    Peony extract quiets redness and evens out tone.

The texture

Cloud-light, then gone.

It lifts from the jar like whipped cream and melts into warm skin in seconds. No residue and no shine, just a soft finish that sits well under makeup.

The ritual

Four steps, one flower.

Start with the cream. Add the serum in the morning, the eye balm before bed and the oil whenever your skin feels thirsty.
